How this race was made

Ten Blender mini-z-sized cars (16–19 cm, different heights, bodies, liveries, a metallic one, a black one, two white look-alikes told apart only by their stripe/roof colour), each rendered crossing the line alone from a camera 1.8 m straight above (480×270 engine resolution, 8 Cycles samples, 1/120 s shutter, 60 fps). The race is composited from those passes: each car has its own pace (17.4–18.6 s laps), ±0.3 s lap noise, 4 % chance of a 2–7 s spin per lap, a rolling start 0.45 s apart. Cars arriving within 0.12 s of each other take neighbouring lanes 10 cm apart, so they cross abreast with about 1 cm between bodies. Every crossing's true time and car are known, which is what the Check cards and the amber/red cells are scored against. Timing is at the front of each car (Settings → Timing → front touches the line), and laps go through the new lap-timing check: a car can't be named twice closer together than it can lap; the weaker of the two goes to a car that is due, or is left for you to check.
